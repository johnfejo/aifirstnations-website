02 · What We Deliver

Five capabilities. One operator.

Every engagement is flown and reported on directly by the Chief Remote Pilot — no subcontracted crews, no generic templates.

01

Drone Survey & Environmental Monitoring

Certified aerial survey for environmental, land management and infrastructure monitoring using a DJI Matrice 4T RTK (thermal + RTK precision).

For: Councils, land councils, ranger groups, private landholders
02

AI Species Classification

Mangrove and vegetation species identification models built with RMIT University, currently running at 97% accuracy (EfficientNet-B0).

For: Environmental agencies, research partners, land managers
03

Cultural Heritage Documentation

Drone-based mapping and documentation of rock art and cultural heritage sites, conducted under appropriate cultural authority and protocol.

For: Traditional Owner groups, heritage bodies
04

Ranger & Biosecurity Support

Aerial support for ranger programs across Cape York and the Torres Strait, including biosecurity and marine debris monitoring.

For: Ranger groups, NRM bodies
05

Training & Education

School and community drone/AI education sessions connecting Indigenous knowledge with emerging technology, ranger and junior ranger skills programs, and pre-deployment / operational drone training for teams and partner organisations.

For: Schools, rangers & junior rangers, disadvantaged youth, community organisations, partner operations teams
