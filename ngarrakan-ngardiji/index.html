Meet the crew

Ngarrakan, the wedge-tailed eagle, sees far, flies high and shares the sky. A good drone pilot does the same. Pick your level above, then work through the six steps and earn your certificate. Every level has the same six steps, from Year 3 all the way to Year 12, and the story grows as Kiki and KK become better drone operators.

How a drone crew works

The instructor, Pop Pop, is in charge. Students under 16 always fly with him watching.

The Pilot, Kiki, flies the drone and keeps it in sight the whole time.

The Spotter, KK, watches the sky for Ngarrakan, other birds and aircraft, and calls "STOP" if anything is unsafe.

The drone flies over Country, at least 30 metres from everyone else, never over people.
