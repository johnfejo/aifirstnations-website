Age Assurance Technology Trial

Whose experience shapes the technology?

The published Age Assurance Technology Trial report identifies John Fejo as a Trial Cultural Advisor. This contribution forms part of John’s experience connecting cultural perspectives with technology evaluation and engagement.

The role belongs to John’s professional background. It does not imply that AI FIRST NATIONS led the national trial or that government endorses its services.

Indigenous Australian Datathon

Community priorities.
New possibilities.

John’s involvement in the Indigenous Australian Datathon includes Aboriginal Ambassador and committee responsibilities, bringing cultural perspectives into a setting where communities and technology specialists work together.

The Datathon connects Indigenous communities with data science and AI to explore challenges on Country. KJR’s published account describes the cultural and technical contribution of the committee and its collaboration with Goondoi.
