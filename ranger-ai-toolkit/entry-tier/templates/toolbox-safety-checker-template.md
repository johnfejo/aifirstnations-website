# Toolbox Safety Checker Template — Ranger AI Toolkit

A pre-task safety checklist in the style of a standard Job Safety Analysis (JSA). Use it at the start of every task, toolbox talk, or shift where the activity, crew, or conditions have changed from the last time this task was run.

**How to use this template:**
- Fill in Part A before the crew starts work — ideally as a group, out loud, not by one person alone at a desk.
- Part B is filled in on the day, task by task.
- Keep completed checklists on file per your program's record-keeping requirements — a funding body or WHS audit may ask to see them.
