# Field Observation Template — Ranger AI Toolkit

A fillable template covering 18 observation categories typical of ranger program fieldwork. Print it, fill it in on paper in the field, and type it up later — or fill it in digitally on a tablet/phone. Either way, it's designed to slot straight into [Prompt 1 or the relevant prompt](../prompt-library.md) in the prompt library afterwards: paste your filled-in notes into the AI prompt and it'll turn them into a finished report.

**How to use this template:**
- You won't fill in all 18 sections on every outing. Use only the categories relevant to what you observed that day.
- Write in short notes, not full sentences — this is a capture tool, not the finished report. The AI prompt library turns these notes into finished documents afterwards.
- If a group without the Ranger Field App is using this as their only record, treat the "General" section on every entry as mandatory even when a specific category section is skipped.
