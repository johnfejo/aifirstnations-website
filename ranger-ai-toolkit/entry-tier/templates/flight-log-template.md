# Drone Flight Log Template (Paper Version) — Ranger AI Toolkit

A general-purpose drone/RPA flight log structure. This template is **not tied to any specific operator's authorisation** — it is a blank structure any ranger group can use under their own CASA authorisation (ReOC, or the relevant excluded-category / sub-2kg rules your program operates under).

**Before you use this template:** confirm what authorisation your program operates under and what record-keeping that authorisation requires. This template gives you a sensible general structure — it does not tell you what CASA requires for your specific operation. If you don't know, check with your program's Chief Remote Pilot or a CASA-accredited advisor before relying on this as your compliance record.

## Pre-Flight Checklist (complete before every flight)

- [ ] Airspace checked (NOTAMs, restricted/controlled zones)
- [ ] Weather conditions within aircraft operating limits
- [ ] Battery(ies) charged and inspected
- [ ] Airframe, propellers, and gimbal inspected for damage
- [ ] Firmware/software up to date
- [ ] Home point and return-to-home settings confirmed
- [ ] Site hazards assessed (people, wildlife, powerlines, terrain)
- [ ] Site-specific approvals confirmed (land access, cultural site protocols, landholder permission)
- [ ] Visual observer briefed, if applicable
- [ ] Emergency procedure confirmed with crew

## Post-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Aircraft inspected for damage post-landing
- [ ] Footage/data downloaded and backed up
- [ ] Any defects or incidents logged in the entry above
- [ ] Battery(ies) stored per manufacturer guidance
- [ ] Flight log entry completed and signed
