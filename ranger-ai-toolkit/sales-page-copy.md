## The problem you're actually managing

You're not managing individual rangers' workloads — you're managing a team's worth of reporting quality, compliance exposure, and onboarding time, all at once. That means:

- **Inconsistent reporting quality across your team.** Some rangers write tight, complete, useful reports. Others leave gaps you have to chase. That inconsistency is a management problem you shouldn't have to solve one ranger at a time.
- **Compliance risk from late or missing logs.** A missing flight log or an incomplete biosecurity entry isn't just untidy — it's exposure, to your program and to you personally as the person accountable for it.
- **Time lost teaching new rangers how to write reports.** Every new ranger costs you hours of your own time before they can file something you don't need to rewrite. That's hours you don't get back, on a cycle that repeats every time you onboard someone new.

None of these are solved by working harder. They're solved by giving your team a faster, more consistent starting point every time they sit down to write something.
